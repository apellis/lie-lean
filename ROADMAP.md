## What Mathlib already provides (at tag v4.34.1)

Use these; do not duplicate them.

  tensor products, semidirect products, extensions: `Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/*`.
- Free Lie algebras `FreeLieAlgebra R X`, with `FreeLieAlgebra.universalEnvelopingEquivFreeAlgebra`
  (the enveloping algebra of a free Lie algebra is the free associative algebra).

  Construction only; nothing is proved about it. Note it has `h` spanned by the `n` elements

  form, semisimple Lie algebras, the root system of a semisimple Lie algebra:
  `Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Weights/*`, `Killing.lean`, `Semisimple/*`.

## Target theorems

For a symmetrizable generalized Cartan matrix `A` and `K` of characteristic zero:

1. **Gabber–Kac** ([Kac] Thm. 9.11; [GK]): the maximal ideal `𝔯` of `𝔤̃(A)` meeting `𝔥` trivially
   is generated by the Serre elements `(ad eᵢ)^{1-aᵢⱼ} eⱼ`, `(ad fᵢ)^{1-aᵢⱼ} fⱼ` (`i ≠ j`). So
   `𝔤(A)` is presented by the Chevalley generators, the relations (1.2.1) below and the Serre
   relations.
2. **Presentation of integrable highest-weight modules** ([Kac] Cor. 10.4): for a
   dominant integral `Λ`, `L(Λ) = M(Λ) / Σᵢ U(𝔫₋) fᵢ^{⟨Λ, αᵢ^∨⟩+1} v_Λ`.
3. **Complete reducibility** ([Kac] Thm. 10.7): every integrable `𝔤(A)`-module in
   category `𝒪` is a direct sum of modules `L(Λ)`, `Λ` dominant integral.
4. **Weyl–Kac character formula** ([Kac] Thm. 10.4) — optional, but the standard route to 2.

Status: all four are proved — 1: `AuxLieAlgebra.maxIdeal_eq_serreIdeal` (M7); 2:
`FPowQuotient.equivIrreducibleModule`; 3: `IsCategoryO.exists_isInternal_irreducibleModule`; 4:
`IrreducibleModule.exp_rho_mul_denominator_mul_character` (M8).

For a Lie algebra `L` over a commutative ring `R` that is free as an `R`-module (at least: over a
field), with an ordered basis `(xⱼ)`:

- the ordered monomials `x_{j₁} ⋯ x_{jₖ}` (`j₁ ≤ ⋯ ≤ jₖ`) form an `R`-basis of `U(L)`;
- equivalently, the symmetrization / the canonical map `Sym(L) → gr U(L)` is an isomorphism;
- consequences: `ι : L → U(L)` is injective; if `L = L₁ ⊕ L₂` as modules with `L₁, L₂`
  subalgebras, multiplication `U(L₁) ⊗ U(L₂) → U(L)` is a linear isomorphism; `U(L)` is a free
  `U(L')`-module for a subalgebra `L'` with a complementary basis.

References: [Hum] §17.3–17.4, [Bou] I §2.7. Several proofs exist (Humphreys' construction of a
representation on `Sym(L)`; Bergman's diamond lemma; the Koszul / Braverman–Gaitsgory approach).
Choose the one that fits Mathlib's `TensorAlgebra`, `SymmetricAlgebra` and `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra`
API best. This milestone is independently valuable for Mathlib.

  linearly independent `Π^∨ = {αᵢ^∨} ⊂ 𝔥` and `Π = {αᵢ} ⊂ 𝔥*` such that `⟨αᵢ^∨, αⱼ⟩ = aᵢⱼ`.
  Existence and uniqueness up to isomorphism ([Kac] Prop. 1.1).
- Root lattice `Q = ⊕ ℤαᵢ`, `Q₊`, height, the partial order on `𝔥*`.

- `𝔤̃(A)`: generated by `eᵢ, fᵢ (i ∈ I)` and `𝔥`, with relations (1.2.1):
  `[eᵢ, fⱼ] = δᵢⱼ αᵢ^∨`, `[h, h'] = 0`, `[h, eᵢ] = ⟨αᵢ, h⟩ eᵢ`, `[h, fᵢ] = -⟨αᵢ, h⟩ fᵢ`.
- [Kac] Thm. 1.2: (a), (b) `𝔤̃ = 𝔫̃₋ ⊕ 𝔥 ⊕ 𝔫̃₊` as vector spaces, with `𝔫̃₊` (resp. `𝔫̃₋`) freely

  basis `v₁ … vₙ`; it uses `FreeLieAlgebra` and its enveloping algebra.)
- `𝔤(A) := 𝔤̃(A) / 𝔯`: triangular and root space decompositions, `𝔥 ↪ 𝔤(A)`, the Chevalley

- The Serre relations hold in `𝔤(A)`: `(ad eᵢ)^{1-aᵢⱼ} eⱼ = 0 = (ad fᵢ)^{1-aᵢⱼ} fⱼ` for `i ≠ j`

- Comparison with Mathlib's `Matrix.ToLieAlgebra`: for nonsingular symmetrizable `A` (e.g. finite
  type) it should be isomorphic to `𝔤(A)` once Gabber–Kac (or, in finite type, Serre's theorem)
  is known; for singular `A` it has a smaller Cartan part and should be compared with the derived
  algebra `𝔤'(A)`. Do this comparison only when it is needed or cheap.

`TitsCone/Finite.lean` proves that finite Weyl group implies full Tits cone, and that fullness
is equivalent to finiteness of the positive real coroots, over arbitrary ordered fields.
`TitsCone/FiniteConverse.lean` proves the converse and
`finite_weylGroup_iff_titsCone_eq_univ`: (e)(i) iff (ii), in the same dual-space convention.
Finiteness follows from an injective finite-valued simple-coroot image tuple; injectivity
uses chamber uniqueness and freeness at a strictly dominant weight on the entire dual space,
not an assumed faithful action on a smaller span. No symmetrizability or extra
finite-dimensionality hypothesis is needed. `KacMoody/FiniteRoots.lean` proves that
finite Weyl group forces every full root to be real, and that full root-system finiteness
is equivalent to Weyl-group finiteness over every characteristic-zero field, including ℂ.
Its reconstructed averaging proof uses positive integer root heights, not an order on
the scalar field. With an ordered field, this also identifies full root-system finiteness
with fullness of the Tits cone. `KacMoody/DualFiniteRoots.lean` constructs the Weyl-group
isomorphism with the transposed realization and proves the full dual-root branch (e)(iv).
It also transports these roots to the original Cartan space via evaluation, without assuming
that the entire Cartan space is isomorphic to its double dual. Thus Proposition 3.12(e)'s
four conditions are equivalent: finite Weyl group, full Tits cone, finite full roots,
and finite full dual roots, with the stated dual-space convention for the cone.
The root/Weyl equivalences hold over arbitrary characteristic-zero fields; the cone
equivalence uses ordered fields. No finite-type or symmetrizability premise is used.
`TitsCone/Interior.lean` proves the finite-stabilizer ⇒ interior direction of Proposition
3.12(f), for points of the actual Tits cone in the finite-dimensional Hausdorff real topology.
It uses the actual Weyl stabilizer and requires cone membership, not merely finite stabilizer.
`TitsCone/InteriorConverse.lean` proves the converse and the combined
`mem_interior_titsCone_iff_finite_stabilizer`, completing Proposition 3.12(f) under these
topology hypotheses. The proof bounds vanishing real coroots by perturbation and proves
stabilizer-tuple injectivity on the entire dual. Cone membership is explicit in the iff;
no finite-Weyl-group or symmetrizability assumption is added.
