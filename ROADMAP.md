# Roadmap

Status markers: `[ ]` not started, `[~]` in progress, `[x]` done (with the main declaration
names). Keep them current.

Primary reference: **[Kac]** V. G. Kac, *Infinite dimensional Lie algebras*, 3rd ed., Cambridge
University Press, 1990. Section and theorem numbers below refer to it and should be re-checked
against the book when it is available; where a number is uncertain it is marked "(check)".

Secondary references:
- **[GK]** O. Gabber, V. G. Kac, *On defining relations of certain infinite-dimensional Lie
  algebras*, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. (N.S.) **5** (1981), 185–189 (open access on ams.org).
- **[KK]** V. G. Kac, D. A. Kazhdan, *Structure of representations with highest weight of

- **[Hum]** J. E. Humphreys, *Introduction to Lie algebras and representation theory*, GTM 9
  (PBW §17, Serre's theorem §18, Weyl's theorem §6, highest-weight theory §20–21).

  with complete proofs, Chapters 14–19).
- **[Bou]** N. Bourbaki, *Lie groups and Lie algebras*, Ch. I §2.7 (PBW).

Throughout, `K` is a field of characteristic zero unless stated otherwise (Kac works over `ℂ`;
all arguments used here work over any field of characteristic zero). Prove results over more
general rings when the argument allows it.

## What Mathlib already provides (at tag v4.34.1)

Use these; do not duplicate them.

- Lie algebras, ideals, quotients, subalgebras, homomorphisms, modules, direct sums, base change,
  tensor products, semidirect products, extensions: `Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/*`.
- Free Lie algebras `FreeLieAlgebra R X`, with `FreeLieAlgebra.universalEnvelopingEquivFreeAlgebra`
  (the enveloping algebra of a free Lie algebra is the free associative algebra).
- Universal enveloping algebra `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra R L` with `ι`, `lift`, `hom_ext`
  (universal property only — **no PBW**).
- `Matrix.ToLieAlgebra R A` (`Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/SerreConstruction.lean`): the free Lie algebra
  on `e_i, f_i, h_i` modulo the Chevalley–Serre relations, for any square integer matrix `A`.
  Construction only; nothing is proved about it. Note it has `h` spanned by the `n` elements
  `h_i` (no realization), so for singular `A` it is not Kac's `𝔤(A)`.
- `sl₂` triples and their basic representation theory: `Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Sl2.lean`.
- Weights and generalized weight spaces for nilpotent Lie algebras, Cartan subalgebras, Killing
  form, semisimple Lie algebras, the root system of a semisimple Lie algebra:
  `Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Weights/*`, `Killing.lean`, `Semisimple/*`.
- Root systems and root pairings, bases, Cartan matrices, Weyl groups:
  `Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/*`; Geck's construction of the semisimple Lie algebra of a
  crystallographic reduced root system (`RootPairing.GeckConstruction.*`).
- Graded Lie algebras (`Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Graded.lean`), Lie bases satisfying Chevalley–Serre
  relations (`Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Basis/*`), loop algebras (`Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Loop.lean`).

Not in Mathlib (checked by searching at v4.34.1): PBW, Verma modules, category `𝒪`, Casimir
operators, contravariant (Shapovalov) forms, Serre's theorem, Weyl's complete reducibility,
generalized Cartan matrices and Kac–Moody algebras.

## Target theorems

For a symmetrizable generalized Cartan matrix `A` and `K` of characteristic zero:

1. **Gabber–Kac** ([Kac] Thm. 9.11; [GK]): the maximal ideal `𝔯` of `𝔤̃(A)` meeting `𝔥` trivially
   is generated by the Serre elements `(ad eᵢ)^{1-aᵢⱼ} eⱼ`, `(ad fᵢ)^{1-aᵢⱼ} fⱼ` (`i ≠ j`). So
   `𝔤(A)` is presented by the Chevalley generators, the relations (1.2.1) below and the Serre
   relations.
2. **Presentation of integrable highest-weight modules** ([Kac] Cor. 10.4 (check)): for a
   dominant integral `Λ`, `L(Λ) = M(Λ) / Σᵢ U(𝔫₋) fᵢ^{⟨Λ, αᵢ^∨⟩+1} v_Λ`.
3. **Complete reducibility** ([Kac] Thm. 10.7 (check)): every integrable `𝔤(A)`-module in
   category `𝒪` is a direct sum of modules `L(Λ)`, `Λ` dominant integral.
4. **Weyl–Kac character formula** ([Kac] Thm. 10.4) — optional, but the standard route to 2.

## Milestones (in dependency order)

### M1. Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt  `[x]`

Done: `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.pbwBasis`, `pbwBasis_apply`, `pbwEquiv` (U(L) ≃ₗ R[X_i]),
`ι_injective` (`LieLean/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/PBW.lean`, Humphreys' proof, over any
commutative ring for `L` with an ordered basis); `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.map`,
`tensorEquivOfIsCompl` (`U(L₁) ⊗ U(L₂) ≃ₗ U(L)` for complementary free subalgebras),
`map_incl_injective_of_isCompl` (`.../TensorDecomposition.lean`);
`FreeLieAlgebra.toFreeAlgebra_injective` (`LieLean/Algebra/Lie/Free.lean`). Not done: the graded
statement `Sym(L) ≅ gr U(L)` (not needed downstream).


For a Lie algebra `L` over a commutative ring `R` that is free as an `R`-module (at least: over a
field), with an ordered basis `(xⱼ)`:

- the ordered monomials `x_{j₁} ⋯ x_{jₖ}` (`j₁ ≤ ⋯ ≤ jₖ`) form an `R`-basis of `U(L)`;
- equivalently, the symmetrization / the canonical map `Sym(L) → gr U(L)` is an isomorphism;
- consequences: `ι : L → U(L)` is injective; if `L = L₁ ⊕ L₂` as modules with `L₁, L₂`
  subalgebras, multiplication `U(L₁) ⊗ U(L₂) → U(L)` is a linear isomorphism; `U(L)` is a free
  `U(L')`-module for a subalgebra `L'` with a complementary basis.

References: [Hum] §17.3–17.4, [Bou] I §2.7. Several proofs exist (Humphreys' construction of a
representation on `Sym(L)`; Bergman's diamond lemma; the Koszul / Braverman–Gaitsgory approach).
Choose the one that fits Mathlib's `TensorAlgebra`, `SymmetricAlgebra` and `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra`
API best. This milestone is independently valuable for Mathlib.

### M2. Generalized Cartan matrices and realizations  `[x]`  ([Kac] §1.1, §2.1)

Done (`LieLean/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Generalized.lean`): `Matrix.IsGeneralizedCartan`,
`Matrix.IsSymmetrizable` (+ `isSymmetrizable_iff_exists_eq_diagonal_mul`, Kac's `A = DB` form),
`Matrix.Realization` (convention `⟨αⱼ, αᵢ^∨⟩ = aᵢⱼ`), existence `Realization.std`, uniqueness
`Realization.exists_linearEquiv` (Prop. 1.1), root lattice `Realization.rootOf` (injective in
characteristic zero). The root lattice is modelled as `ι → ℤ`, `Q₊ \ 0` as `posCone`.


- A generalized Cartan matrix (GCM) `A = (aᵢⱼ)_{i,j ∈ I}`, `I` finite, `aᵢⱼ ∈ ℤ`: `aᵢᵢ = 2`,
  `aᵢⱼ ≤ 0` for `i ≠ j`, `aᵢⱼ = 0 ↔ aⱼᵢ = 0`. Relate to Mathlib's `CartanMatrix` material and
  `RootPairing.cartanMatrix` where possible.
- Symmetrizable: `A = D B` with `D` diagonal with positive entries and `B` symmetric (rational).
- A realization of `A` ([Kac] §1.1): a `K`-vector space `𝔥` of dimension `2|I| - rank A` with
  linearly independent `Π^∨ = {αᵢ^∨} ⊂ 𝔥` and `Π = {αᵢ} ⊂ 𝔥*` such that `⟨αᵢ^∨, αⱼ⟩ = aᵢⱼ`.
  Existence and uniqueness up to isomorphism ([Kac] Prop. 1.1).
- Root lattice `Q = ⊕ ℤαᵢ`, `Q₊`, height, the partial order on `𝔥*`.

### M3. The algebras 𝔤̃(A) and 𝔤(A)  `[x]`  ([Kac] §1.2–1.3, §1.5 (check))

Done (`LieLean/Algebra/Lie/KacMoody/`): `Matrix.Realization.AuxLieAlgebra` (𝔤̃(A), universal
property `AuxLieAlgebra.lift`, Chevalley involution), Thm. 1.2 via the tensor-algebra
representation: `triangularEquiv` (𝔤̃ = 𝔫̃₋ ⊕ 𝔥 ⊕ 𝔫̃₊, 𝔫̃± free), `h_injective`,
root space decomposition (`rootSpace_zero`, `rootSpace_pos_le`, `finiteDimensional_rootSpace`,
`rootSpace_root`, `rootSpace_nsmul_root_eq_bot`), `maxIdeal` (= 𝔯, `le_maxIdeal_iff`,
`maxIdeal_eq`); `Matrix.Realization.KacMoodyAlgebra` (𝔤(A)) with `triangular_eq_zero`,
`rootSpace_eq_map`, `finrank_rootSpace_root`, `chevalleyInvolution`, and the Serre relations
`serre_e`, `serre_f` (from `AuxLieAlgebra.serreIdeal_le_maxIdeal`, Kac Lemma 1.5 argument).
The comparison with `Matrix.ToLieAlgebra` is not done.


- `𝔤̃(A)`: generated by `eᵢ, fᵢ (i ∈ I)` and `𝔥`, with relations (1.2.1):
  `[eᵢ, fⱼ] = δᵢⱼ αᵢ^∨`, `[h, h'] = 0`, `[h, eᵢ] = ⟨αᵢ, h⟩ eᵢ`, `[h, fᵢ] = -⟨αᵢ, h⟩ fᵢ`.
- [Kac] Thm. 1.2: (a) `𝔤̃ = 𝔫̃₋ ⊕ 𝔥 ⊕ 𝔫̃₊` as vector spaces, with `𝔫̃₊` (resp. `𝔫̃₋`) freely
  generated by the `eᵢ` (resp. `fᵢ`); (b) root space decomposition
  `𝔫̃₊ = ⊕_{α ∈ Q₊ \ 0} 𝔤̃_α` with `dim 𝔤̃_α < ∞`; (c) among ideals of `𝔤̃` meeting `𝔥` trivially
  there is a unique maximal one, `𝔯`, and `𝔯 = (𝔯 ∩ 𝔫̃₋) ⊕ (𝔯 ∩ 𝔫̃₊)`.
  (The standard proof builds a representation of `𝔤̃` on the tensor algebra `T(V)`, `V` with
  basis `v₁ … vₙ`; it uses `FreeLieAlgebra` and its enveloping algebra.)
- `𝔤(A) := 𝔤̃(A) / 𝔯`: triangular and root space decompositions, `𝔥 ↪ 𝔤(A)`, the Chevalley
  involution, `dim 𝔤_{±αᵢ} = 1`, `𝔤_{kαᵢ} = 0` for `|k| ≥ 2`.
- The Serre relations hold in `𝔤(A)`: `(ad eᵢ)^{1-aᵢⱼ} eⱼ = 0 = (ad fᵢ)^{1-aᵢⱼ} fⱼ` for `i ≠ j`
  ([Kac] §3.3 (check); the Serre elements generate an ideal of `𝔤̃` meeting `𝔥` trivially).
- Comparison with Mathlib's `Matrix.ToLieAlgebra`: for nonsingular symmetrizable `A` (e.g. finite
  type) it should be isomorphic to `𝔤(A)` once Gabber–Kac (or, in finite type, Serre's theorem)
  is known; for singular `A` it has a smaller Cartan part and should be compared with the derived
  algebra `𝔤'(A)`. Do this comparison only when it is needed or cheap.

### M7. Gabber–Kac  `[ ]`  ([Kac] Thm. 9.11; [GK])

Statement: target theorem 1. Rough outline only — reconstruct the argument from [Kac] §9.11 /
[GK] and write it out in full in the module docstring before formalizing; do not trust the
details below: let `𝔰₊ ⊂ 𝔯₊ = 𝔯 ∩ 𝔫̃₊` be the ideal generated by the positive Serre elements; if

satisfying the constraints from integrability (use `W`-invariance). Write out the argument in the
module docstring before formalizing it.

### M9 (optional). Finite type  `[ ]`

For a Cartan matrix of finite type: `𝔤(A)` is finite-dimensional semisimple and agrees with
